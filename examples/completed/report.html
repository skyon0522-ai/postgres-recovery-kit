Scope and limits

This review reads selected local receipt and stdout files only. It makes no live database connection. The historical evidence is editable and unauthenticated; matching hashes do not authenticate it. This synthetic network-fault rehearsal does not establish backup recovery, power-loss safety, general exactly-once delivery or production readiness.

On a narrow screen, focus a table and use the arrow keys, or scroll it sideways. The scope remains visible when printed.
